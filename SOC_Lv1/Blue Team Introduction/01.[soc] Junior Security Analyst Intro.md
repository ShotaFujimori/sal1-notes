# 50.SAL1 — Junior Security Analyst Intro — Cheat Sheet

**Focus:** Roles within a Security Operations Center (SOC), daily duties of a Tier 1 Analyst, and basic alert handling workflow.

## TL;DR
[cite_start]As a **Junior Security Analyst (SOC Level 1)**, you are the first line of defense working in a 24/7 shift environment[cite: 10, 22]. Your primary goal is to triage incoming alerts: determining if they are false alarms or real threats. [cite_start]You do not fix the tools or manage the department; you investigate, block simple threats, and escalate complex incidents to Senior Analysts[cite: 38].

## Daily Workflow & Operations
In this TryHackMe module, "commands" are actions performed within the simulated SOC Dashboard.

### 1. Intelligence Gathering
**Action:** Review Cyber News daily.
**Why:** Threat groups and vulnerabilities evolve constantly. [cite_start]You must know what is trending (e.g., Supply Chain attacks, DDoS)[cite: 8, 9].

* **Recent Examples (from text):**
    * [cite_start]*Salesloft Breach:* Linked to GitHub compromise[cite: 11].
    * [cite_start]*Outlook Backdoor:* "NotDoor" deployed by Russian APT28[cite: 13, 16].
    * [cite_start]*DDoS:* Cloudflare blocked an 11.5 Tbps attack[cite: 17].
    * [cite_start]*Supply Chain:* Compromised `npm` packages[cite: 19].

### 2. Alert Investigation (Network)
**Action:** Identify malicious Traffic.
**Scenario:** An alert indicates suspicious network connections.
**Execution:**
1.  Check the alert details for Source/Destination IPs.
2.  Compare against Threat Intelligence (in the lab, this is implied).
3.  **Block** or **Escalate**.

* **Lab Example:**
    * [cite_start]**Malicious IP Found:** `221.181.185.159`[cite: 74].
    * **Action Taken:** Block IP on Firewall.
    * [cite_start]**Result (Flag):** `THM{until-we-meet-again}`[cite: 76].

### 3. Alert Investigation (Phishing)
**Action:** Analyze Email Headers and Body.
**Scenario:** "Email Marked as Phishing after Delivery" (See provided image).
**Analysis Checklist:**
* **Urgency:** "600% price increase", "urgent notice" (Social Engineering) [Image analysis].
* **Attachment:** `REPORT.rar` (Archives are suspicious in business context) [Image analysis].
* **Auth Checks:** `SPF/Fail; DKIM/Fail` (Indicates spoofing) [Image analysis].

**Verdict:** **True Positive**.

### 4. Escalation
**Action:** Hand over to Tier 2.
**When:** When the attack is too complex or requires deeper forensics than a Tier 1 scope allows.
**Command/Action:**
* [cite_start]**Escalate To:** Will Griffin (Senior Analyst)[cite: 75].

## Gotchas & Pitfalls
* [cite_start]**Role Confusion:** Do not try to reconfigure the SIEM or firewall rules yourself unless authorized; that is the **SOC Engineer's** (Corey) job, not the Analyst's[cite: 41].
* **Alert Fatigue:** You may be "buried under a mountain of tickets." [cite_start]Prioritization is key[cite: 65].
* **Isolation:** You are not alone. If unsure, ask the Senior Analyst. [cite_start]Do not guess on a verdict[cite: 33, 38].
