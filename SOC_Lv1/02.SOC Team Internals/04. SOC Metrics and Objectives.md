# 57.SLA1 — SOC Metrics and Objectives — Cheat Sheet

**Focus:** Measuring SOC efficiency using standard metrics (MTTD, MTTA, MTTR) and methods to improve them (SLA compliance, False Positive Remediation).

## TL;DR

* **Core Goal:** Detect threats fast, acknowledge them instantly, and remediate them completely.
* **The "Mean" Times:**
* **MTTD (Detect):** Speed of your *tools* finding the threat.
* **MTTA (Acknowledge):** Speed of *L1 Analysts* picking up the ticket.
* **MTTR (Respond):** Speed of the *team* stopping the attack.


* **SLA (Service Level Agreement):** The contract defining acceptable speeds (e.g., "Acknowledge criticals in 15 mins").
* **False Positives:** High noise (>80%) kills productivity. Fix it by tuning rules or automating closure.

---

## 1. Key Performance Metrics (The Formulas)

**Concept:** You can't improve what you don't measure. These 3 metrics define SOC success.

### Metric: MTTD (Mean Time to Detect)

* **Definition:** Time between the *Attack Start*  *Alert Created*.
* **Good Target:** < 5 minutes (Real-time).
* **Execution/Fix:** If high, **Tune Detection Rules** (e.g., check if SIEM logs are delayed or rules run too infrequently).
* **Example:** Attacker runs malware at 08:00. SIEM alerts at 08:12. MTTD = 12 mins.

### Metric: MTTA (Mean Time to Acknowledge)

* **Definition:** Time between *Alert Created*  *Analyst status "In Progress"*.
* **Good Target:** < 10-15 minutes.
* **Execution/Fix:** If high, **Tune Notifications** (e.g., enable PagerDuty/SMS for criticals) or **Load Balance** analysts.
* **Example:** Alert appears at 08:12. L1 Analyst clicks "In Progress" at 08:22. MTTA = 10 mins.

### Metric: MTTR (Mean Time to Respond)

* **Definition:** Time between *Alert Created*  *Threat Contained/Remediated*.
* **Good Target:** < 60 minutes.
* **Execution/Fix:** If high, **Create Workbooks** (Playbooks) so analysts don't waste time guessing what to do.
* **Example:** Analyst starts at 08:22. Malware is cleaned/Host isolated at 09:13. MTTR = 51 mins (Total time since 08:22).

## 2. Operational Metrics (Health Checks)

**Concept:** Metrics that indicate if the SOC team is drowning or healthy.

### Metric: False Positive Rate (FPR)

* **Formula:** `False Positives / Total Alerts`
* **Target:** < 50% (Ideal), **Avoid > 80%**.
* **Impact:** High FPR causes "Alert Fatigue" (analysts ignore real threats).
* **Execution/Fix:** **Whitelisting** (Exclude trusted updates/scanners) or **SOAR** (Auto-close known safe signals).

### Metric: Threat Detection Rate (TDR)

* **Formula:** `Detected Threats / Total Real Threats`
* **Target:** **100%**.
* **Impact:** Anything less than 100% means a breach was missed.
* **Execution/Fix:** **Threat Hunting** and continuous rule updates.

## 3. Practical Scenarios & Lab Flags

**Concept:** Applying the metrics to solve management complaints.

**Scenario 1: Slow Response (High MTTR)**

* **Complaint:** "Took 6 hours to kick the hacker out."
* **Diagnosis:** Analysts didn't know how to reset MFA/Passwords quickly.
* **Action:** Create a **Credential Rotation Workbook**.
* **Flag:** `THM{mttr:quick_start_but_slow_response}`

**Scenario 2: Slow Detection (High MTTD)**

* **Complaint:** "Attack happened hours before we knew."
* **Diagnosis:** Detection rules are lagging.
* **Action:** Task SOC Engineer to **fix SIEM ingestion delay**.
* **Flag:** `THM{mttd:time_between_attack_and_alert}`

**Scenario 3: Analyst Burnout (High FPR)**

* **Complaint:** "Analysts are tired and missing alerts."
* **Diagnosis:** Too much noise (False Positives).
* **Action:** Task SOC Engineer to **tune out noise/whitelisting**.
* **Flag:** `THM{fpr:the_main_cause_of_11_burnout}`

## Gotchas & Pitfalls

* **The "Zero Alert" Trap:** Seeing 0 alerts for a month isn't "secure"; it usually means your SIEM is broken or blind. This is a **Nay** (Bad sign).
* **SLA Weekends:** If your SLA is "8/5" (Mon-Fri) and an alert comes in on Saturday, the "clock" doesn't start until **Monday morning**. (Attackers love this gap).
* **Calculation:** MTTR usually includes the *entire* lifecycle from detection to close. Don't confuse it with just the "remediation phase."
* *Example Calculation:*
1. Attack: 12:00
2. Alert: 12:12 (MTTD: 12m)
3. Triage Start: 12:22 (MTTA: 10m)
4. Remediation Done: 13:13
5. Total MTTR: 12:22 to 13:13 = **51 minutes** (from alert acknowledgment to finish).
