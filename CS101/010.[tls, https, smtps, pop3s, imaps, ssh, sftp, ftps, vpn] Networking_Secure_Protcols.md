## TL;DR
- tls upgrades plaintext protocols to provide **confidentiality, integrity, authenticity** without changing IP/TCP or the app protocol itself. (http→https, smtp→smtps, pop3→pop3s, imap→imaps)
- ssh replaced telnet for secure remote access; it also supports **tunneling** and **file transfer (sftp)**.
- ftps ≠ sftp (tls vs ssh). vpn provides encrypted tunnels that can link sites or route all traffic.

## Gotchas
- **Self-signed certificates** don’t prove server authenticity; prefer CA-signed (Let’s Encrypt available). :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}  
- Secure defaults/ports: **HTTPS 443, SMTPS 465/587, POP3S 995, IMAPS 993**; plain-text ports: **80/25/110/143**. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}  
- **SFTP (over SSH/22) ≠ FTPS (FTP over TLS/21 or 990)**; data-channel/NAT handling differs. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}  
- VPNs may route **all** traffic through the tunnel (check DNS leaks/geo-IP); laws vary by country. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}
