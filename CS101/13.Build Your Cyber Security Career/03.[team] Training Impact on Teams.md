
# 46. SAL1 — Build Your Cyber Security Career: **Training Impact on Teams** — Cheat Sheet

## TL;DR

Focused, hands-on training boosts team productivity (example used: **+4%**), lets you ramp juniors faster, reduces senior coaching load, standardizes skill baselines, and scales capacity without new headcount. For large orgs, customize content (e.g., **Content Studio**) and integrate via SSO/APIs. A simple ROI model routinely shows **triple- to six-fold** returns. 

---

## 1) Why Training Matters (Ops & Capacity)

* **Practice before crisis:** safer to learn in labs than during live incidents.
* **Capacity without hiring:** trained teams handle more work with the same headcount.
* **Onboarding:** juniors ramp quickly; seniors spend less time re-teaching.
* **Common baseline:** consistent skills assessment across the org improves tasking & career paths.
* **Team cohesion:** CTFs and group learning strengthen collaboration.

## 3) ROI Model (Show the Money)

**Model inputs**

* `N` = employees trained
* `C` = annual fully-loaded cost per employee (USD)
* `p` = productivity uplift (e.g., 0.04 for 4%)
* `T` = training cost per employee (USD)

**Formulas**

* **Savings (annual):** `S = N * C * p`
* **Cost:** `K = N * T`
* **ROI:** `ROI = S / K` (express as %)

**Worked example from room:**

* `N=10, C=$80,000, p=0.04 → S=$32,000`
* `T=$500 → K=$5,000`
* `ROI = 32,000 / 5,000 = 640%` 

### One-liners

```bash
# Bash: compute savings and ROI
N=10; C=80000; p=0.04; T=500
S=$(python3 - <<PY
N=${N}; C=${C}; p=${p}; T=${T}
S=N*C*p; K=N*T; print(int(S), int((S/K)*100))
PY
)
echo "Savings=$$(echo $S | awk '{print $1}')  ROI=$$(echo $S | awk '{print $2}')%"
```

```python
# Python (re-usable): roi.py
def roi(n, cost_per_head, uplift, train_cost):
    savings = n * cost_per_head * uplift
    spend   = n * train_cost
    return {"savings": savings, "roi_pct": 100 * savings / spend}

print(roi(20, 50000, 0.04, 500))  # {'savings': 40000.0, 'roi_pct': 400.0}
```

## 4) Writing a Training Proposal (Template Sketch)

* **Problem:** incident risk, backlog, senior bottlenecks.
* **Solution:** role-based paths, labs, CTFs, assessments.
* **Financials:** include the **Savings / ROI** table above.
* **Roadmap:** 90-day pilot → scale to whole org.

Ask: audience & roles, prior industry experience, content breadth/depth/quality, hands-on labs on one platform, SSO/APIs, analytics, and **total impact vs cost** (training cost is usually dwarfed by productivity gains).

## 6) Team Games & Culture

* Schedule quarterly **CTF days**; rotate captains; post-mortem + share write-ups.
* Reward completions (badges, internal certs).
* Tie training to promotion paths. 

---

## Gotchas (What to Watch For)

* **Measuring the uplift:** define baselines; otherwise ROI looks “hand-wavy.”
* **One-and-done syndrome:** skills decay without a cadence; enforce recurring labs.
* **Generic content mismatch:** without customization, teams may not see workflow relevance.
* **Integration friction:** lack of SSO/APIs kills adoption; test this early.
* **No time allocated:** protect calendar time, or completion rates plummet.
